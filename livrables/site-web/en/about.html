About Us

Tamari & Léo

Two different outlooks. One shared direction.

We are Tamari and Léo — mother and son. Two different generations, two different sets of experience, two different outlooks, but one shared belief: that every person carries within them a unique ability to create something valuable — for themselves and for the world around them.

Tamari &

1978 · Tbilisi → France

I was born in Tbilisi, in the former Soviet Union, on December 7, 1978. I had a happy, carefree childhood, full of love. From an early age I was drawn to anything connected to creation and discovery.

I loved books, history, ancient civilizations, art, and astronomy. I dreamed about photography. I tried making my own clothes by hand. I was always curious about how the world worked and what drove it.

Life took me in a different direction. In 1991, a difficult period began in Georgia. In 1994, our family left the country — we lived in Belgium first, then, in 1998, we arrived in France.

A new country, a new language, a new culture, a new life. Everything had to start over from zero.

That road is what led me to the profession of translator. I spent more than 15 years as an official interpreter within the French court, administrative, and asylum systems. Over those years, I heard the stories of thousands of people.

Alongside that work, I never stopped learning and growing. I explored psychology, coaching, hidden bodies of knowledge — anything that could help me understand the truth of a person's inner world. Deep down, I always knew that being a translator wasn't my final destination. It was a bridge — one that gave me knowledge, experience, and the ability to see people, and their struggles, more deeply.

Along the way I saw both success and disappointment. Courage and fear. The pain of losing my beloved father, and the strength to start over — and I also found a great love, one that changed my life forever.

A person's greatest wealth is the life they've lived and the experience they carry.

& Léo

2000 · France · Builder

Léo Jasarevic was born in France, near Lyon, on December 8, 2000. From childhood he stood out for his curiosity, independent thinking, and drive to create. He was always interested in how things, systems, and mechanisms worked underneath the surface.

Even as a child, he wanted to change anything he didn't like. He started playing tennis at age 6, growing up speaking Georgian, surrounded by his mother, grandparents, and cousins.

At just 12 years old, he was already trying to build his own brand. He built a prototype of a video game platform. He worked on a concept for electric vehicle rental — before anything like it existed yet.

Technology has never been just a tool to him — he sees it as possibility. He believes that technology, used the right way, can simplify people's everyday lives and open up new opportunities. That belief is where his projects are born.

What unites us

Despite our differences in age, experience, and interests, we're alike in a lot of ways.

We love working on ideas, growing, and searching for new paths.

We don't build projects just for the sake of business. We don't want success at any cost.

Some of our projects are about immigration. Some are about technology. Some are about personal growth. But they all share one foundation — the desire to build something rooted in trust, responsibility, and real value.

What this platform is for

This space was created to share our experience, our knowledge, our projects, our discoveries, and the lessons life has taught us.

Here you'll find stories, articles, videos, ideas, and our projects. And everything we build along the way.

This is a place where experience meets innovation. Where the past meets the future, where human stories meet technology, and where human values remain at the center.

Our principle

To us, trust is the greatest responsibility there is. We're aware that every word we write, every idea we share, and every recommendation we make has an impact on real people.

We don't like loud promises.
We don't like misleading people.
We don't like giving advice we're not confident in ourselves.

When we talk about experience, we're talking about what we've actually lived through. When we share knowledge, we try to root it in real practice. We're not looking for perfection — we want to be honest.

What we want to leave behind

We don't know how big our projects will become. We don't know how many people our voice will reach. But we know one thing — a person can create something that outlives their own life and becomes valuable to others.

We want to leave behind something that helps people.
Something that inspires.
Something that makes their path a little easier.
Something that helps them see their own potential.

This is the road we're walking today, and we're glad to have you with us on it.

If our experience helps even one person build a better life for themselves, our work will already have been worth it.
